What is “somatic healing”, anyways??

Somatic healing is a way of working with the trauma we hold in the body. It’s a body-centered approach that bridges the gap between the mind and the physical form to resolve deep-rooted stress.

Rather than just analyzing your experiences intellectually, we use physiological awareness to release the stored, subconscious tension that disrupts your nervous system.

Why not talk therapy?

Talk therapy is incredibly valuable for cognitive understanding, but you cannot talk your way out of a physical response. When we experience stress, our bodies learn to protect us by storing that energy as physical tension. The way I like to describe this is as an “internal flinch”. If someone’s been hit, what will they do the next time someone raises a fist? It’s quite likely that they’ll flinch—and something similar happens every time you experience long-term stress, though the “flinch” may not always be visible. We carry tension inside of us for years, usually unknowingly. This could look like shallow breathing resulting in a constricted diaphragm, or bracing in your connective tissue that leads to chronic pain. This type of internal tension can also lead to poor gut health, and even digestive issues!

Therapy is a useful tool, but I have more freedom to help you heal mind, body, and soul in a somatic healing space. I’ve always been a healer first, and a therapist second. :)

Modern medicine often treats us like a bag of disassembled parts, but our brains are (and always have been) a literal part of our bodies. If you only process trauma intellectually, you leave the physical root cause untouched. Somatic work brings these unconscious physical patterns into conscious awareness, moving you from a state of chronic defensive bracing into a true flow state.

Somatic Healing and Nervous System Regulation

Complex trauma, developmental stress, and high-stimulus modern environments fundamentally alter autonomic nervous system wiring. Stuck in states of high sympathetic activation (chronic fight or flight) or dorsal vagal immobilization (systemic shutdown and freeze), the body defaults into protective bracing patterns. This displays physically as altered breath cycles, neuromuscular holding habits, and lingering myofascial constriction.

Somatic healing is the targeted process of re-establishing somatic mapping and down-regulating hyper-aroused states. By working through a bottom-up framework, we safely resolve somatic dissociation—where the brain fragments away from real-time physical feedback—and rebuild neural safety, expanding your baseline resilience and capacity for regulation from the inside out.
